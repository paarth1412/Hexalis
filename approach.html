Principles

What we hold ourselves to.

P1

Accountability is not shared

One relationship owner across every pillar you use. When something goes wrong there is no round of emails establishing whose problem it is.

P2

Strategy that survives contact

We connect recommendation to execution. If we propose it, we are prepared to be in the room when it is built.

P3

Asset-light where it should be

We own the client, concept, project and quality bar. Production infrastructure, manufacturing and fulfilment come from a vetted partner ecosystem.

P4

Evidence before capital

Particularly in commerce: validate demand at small cost, then commit. Spend on evidence, not on optimism.

P5

Built for the second year

Retainers, managed services and embedded talent are not upsells. They are the point — the relationship should be worth more in year three than in year one.

P6

We will say no

Six pillars is a capability, not a quota. If a pillar does not apply to your problem, we will leave it out of the proposal.
